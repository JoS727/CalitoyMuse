The Debut Collection

Lookbook / Season One

Eight looks composed in shadow, wine, and gold. The first chapter of Calitoy Muse: evening wear and lingerie cut for the body that refuses to be ignored, photographed in the half-light between dusk and disclosure.

Look 01
Nocturne in Wine

A floor-length slip in mulberry silk charmeuse, cut on the bias so it clings and releases with every step. Lace trim at the hem, hand-finished in old gold thread.

Look 02
Gilded Hour

A bralette and high-waisted brief set in antique gold mesh, embroidered with a constellation of seed pearls across the ribs.

Look 03
Ember

A long-line corset in deep oxblood satin, boned in steel and laced up the back. Worn here over a sheer chiffon skirt.

Look 04
Nude & Rose

A soft-cup bra and tap short in nude silk, layered under a rose-toned robe of sheer organza that trails to the floor.

Look 05
The Long Goodbye

A backless gown in jet black velvet, slit to the upper thigh, with a train that sweeps a full metre behind. Photographed in motion against bare plaster.

“Lingerie is not what you take off. It is what you keep on when the room has emptied and the candles are low.”
Joseph Calitoy, Creative Director
Look 06
Vespers

A chemise in smoke-grey silk with a deep lace yoke, photographed against a window at the hour the sky turns the colour of a bruise.

Look 07
Aureate

A bustier in gold brocade with a boned bodice and a peplum hem, paired with matching high-cut briefs. The collection’s most architectural piece.

Look 08
Last Light

A wrap robe in dusty rose velvet over a sheer teddy, belted at the waist with a self-fabric tie. The closing look of Season One.
