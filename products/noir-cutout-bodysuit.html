Noir Cutout Bodysuit

$185.00

Free shipping on orders over $200

The Noir Cutout Bodysuit is bias-cut from 16mm silk satin that drapes without clinging, falls without holding. The square neckline is traced with a hand-sewn lace trim that softens the edge between skin and fabric.

The strappy details cross at the back, creating a geometric pattern against the skin that shifts with movement. The snap closure at the base is flat and gold-plated, invisible beneath fitted clothing.

A bodysuit that reads as lace first and as architecture second. The embroidery does the work of design. The body does the work of display.

Material
82% polyamide, 18% elastane. Black Chantilly lace with hollow-out embroidery and elastic strapping.
Care
Hand wash cold. Lay flat to dry. Do not wring.
Origin
Handcrafted in Paris, France. Calitoy Muse Atelier.
Fit
True to size. Adjustable straps. Snap closure at base.
