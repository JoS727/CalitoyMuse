Noir Embroidered Bodysuit

$195.00

Free shipping on orders over $200

The Noir Embroidered Bodysuit is bias-cut from 16mm silk satin that drapes without clinging, falls without holding. The square neckline is traced with a hand-sewn lace trim that softens the edge between skin and fabric.

The straps are thin and adjustable, finished with the same gold-plated hardware used across the collection. The back is cut low enough to reveal the lace pattern continuing across the shoulder blades.

A bodysuit that reads as lace first and as structure second. The embroidery does the work of design, the body does the work of display.

Material
68% polyamide, 32% silk. Floral Chantilly lace with hollow-out embroidery.
Care
Hand wash cold. Lay flat to dry. Do not twist or wring.
Origin
Handcrafted in Paris, France. Calitoy Muse Atelier.
Fit
True to size. Thin adjustable straps. Model is 178cm and wears size S.
